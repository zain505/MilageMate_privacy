MileageMate Policy

Privacy Policy for MileageMate

MileageMate is an offline app for tracking fuel refills, mileage, and vehicle expenses.

Effective date: May 7, 2026

Information the app stores

MileageMate stores the fuel entries and app settings that you enter directly on your device. This may include items such as odometer readings, fuel quantity, fuel price, notes, currency preference, and distance-unit preference.

How data is used

This information is used only to provide app features such as fuel history, mileage calculations, expense summaries, reports, and data export.

Data sharing

MileageMate does not require an account and does not upload your data to the developer's servers.

If you use the export feature, the app creates a file on your device and shares it only when you choose to use your device's share options.

Data retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device unless you delete it. You can delete entries inside the app, remove exported files from your device, or uninstall the app to remove locally stored app data.

Security

MileageMate is designed to keep your app data on your device. However, no device or software environment can guarantee absolute security.

Children's privacy

MileageMate is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.
